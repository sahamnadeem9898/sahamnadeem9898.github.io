MLOps pipeline and inference cost

10Pearls · same FinTech client
Problem
Shipping a new model took two weeks of manual steps, and inference costs were growing with usage.
What I built
End-to-end MLOps covering data versioning, experiment tracking, a model registry, automated retraining, canary releases and drift monitoring. For serving, I added quantization, request batching and GPU autoscaling on containerized infrastructure.
Result
Model releases cut from 2 weeks to 2–3 days, and inference cost down 25–40%.
